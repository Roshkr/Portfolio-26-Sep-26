# Roushan Kumar - UX/UI and Product Designer

Roushan Kumar is a UX/UI and product designer based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. His portfolio focuses on SaaS, B2B, fintech, enterprise workflows, and mobile experiences. His work includes user research, information architecture, interaction design, prototyping, and design systems.

## Selected projects

### Roushani - B2B SaaS invoicing

[Open the Roushani case study](/#case-study-roushani)

Roushani is a mobile-first invoicing product for service businesses. It aims to reduce repetitive invoice entry and help businesses create professional, branded invoices. The case study covers product strategy, user research, information architecture, invoice creation, customer and service reuse, branding, PDF export, and the design and implementation process.

### Umbrella - enterprise third-party onboarding

[Open the Umbrella case study](/#case-study-umbrella)

This project refined an NDA onboarding form used to add a new third party. Five participants took part in 20-minute sessions. 80% had used an onboarding tool before, participants reported understanding the field meanings, and the average form experience rating was 3.5 out of 5. Feedback identified a confusing company or individual row, a dated visual style, and too many fields. The case study documents the research questions, mind map, participant feedback, implementation, and before-and-after form.

### Bridge2Business - B2B website redesign

[Open the Bridge2Business case study](/#case-study-bridge2business)

A B2B website redesign focused on clearer information hierarchy, UX strategy, responsive design, and communicating the client’s services and value proposition.

### Maxlence - HR and project management SaaS

[Open the Maxlence case study](/#case-study-maxlence)

An HR and project management product covering team workflows, onboarding, project tracking, attendance, and client deliverables. The design work focused on onboarding architecture, role-aware flows, and dashboard customization.

### Naie - salon appointment and discovery platform

[Open the Naie case study](/#case-study-naie)

A concept for a local salon booking platform connecting customer discovery and appointment scheduling with salon operations. The work explores appointment availability, queue transparency, staff selection, and owner-side management.
